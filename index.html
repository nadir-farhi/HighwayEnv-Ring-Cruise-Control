Cruise control on a ring road

Interactive simulators accompanying the paper Cruise control on a ring road in a cumulative-flow traffic model: limit flow, transient regime, calibration and microscopic test.

Macroscopic models

KW, DKW, CC and DCC on a ring of sections: choose the number and length of sections and the damping weight, and watch the ring, the cumulated flows, the densities and the space-time diagram.

Microscopic ring (HighwayEnv)

HighwayEnv's IDM and MOBIL drivers on a multi-lane ring, or on the 260 m ring of Stern et al. (2018), with a speed cap, FollowerStopper, PI with saturation or ACC, at any share of equipped vehicles.
Nadir Farhi, Université Gustave Eiffel. Both simulators run entirely in the browser.

About the models

Cumulated flow qi(t) = cars that entered section i by time t; Ni = ni + qi − qi+1 cars are in section i. With free places n̄i = nmax − ni:

q_i(t) = (1-α) q_i(t-Δt) + α min{ q_{i-1}(t - l/v) + n_{i-1}, [ q_{i-1}(t - l/v̄) + n_{i-1} ] (CC on), q_{i+1}(t - l/w) + n̄_i }

α = 1 gives KW (CC off) or CC (CC on); α < 1 gives DKW or DCC. Damping adds a relaxation time ε = Δt(1−α)/α on every arc, and the limit flow is the minimum over the forward ring, the backward ring and the two-arc circuits of tokens / (delay + ε × arcs).

Delays are whole numbers of time steps; values that do not fall on the grid are rounded, and the panel says so. The cars on the ring are drawn from the cumulated flows by Newell's construction: car c is where the cumulative count N(x,t) equals c, so cars are evenly spread inside each section and move exactly as the flows say.
